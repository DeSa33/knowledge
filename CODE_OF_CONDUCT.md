# Community conduct

Treat contributors respectfully and engage with the substance of their arguments. Critique claims, assumptions, proofs, and measurements rather than personal characteristics.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, deliberate plagiarism, and knowingly fabricated evidence are unacceptable. Preserve intellectual disagreement: an unconventional proposal deserves a clear statement and an appropriate test, while every result remains accountable to evidence.

The maintainer, DeSa33, may edit or remove disruptive material and restrict participation. When practical, explain moderation in terms of the affected conduct. Do not post private information while reporting a concern. Use an existing appropriate GitHub contact route for private concerns; no separate private reporting address is advertised here.
