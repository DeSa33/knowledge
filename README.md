## What I'm working on

My first upload is **Broad Semantic Generalization (BSG)**. I'm exploring how a system can use something it learned in one environment to make better decisions in another, even when the two represent things differently.

The v4 paper lays out eight mathematical problems and a joint challenge. The full problem remains open. The [BSG page](papers/bsg/README.md) has the paper, a reading guide, and the current research questions.

This repo is also where I'll share work from other projects. Each one will have its own files and context.

## Feedback

If you find a mistake, a gap in an argument, or a connection worth exploring, [open an issue](https://github.com/DeSa33/knowledge/issues/new/choose). Point me to the part you're looking at and explain what you found. A counterexample, derivation, or test I can run gives me something concrete to work with.

I'm interested in what holds up when an idea is tested, where it breaks, and what can be improved from there.

[Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) covers proposed changes. To cite a paper, use its title and version; citation details are on the paper's page. Reuse permissions are in [RIGHTS.md](RIGHTS.md).
